Dashboard Stakeholder Stories

City Ops Lead
Headline:
Bengaluru has the highest SLA breach rate at 16.04%.
Evidence:
Bengaluru recorded 17 SLA breaches across 106 bookings, giving it a 16.04% breach rate. The next highest city is Hyderabad at 14.29%, so Bengaluru is 1.75 percentage points higher.
Implication:
For the City Ops lead, Bengaluru is the clearest location to examine for SLA performance because it has the highest share of bookings resulting in an SLA breach.

Category Lead
Headline:
Deep Home Cleaning is the strongest category, while Salon for Women is comparatively under booked.
Evidence:
Deep Home Cleaning generated ₹509,164 in revenue from 176 bookings, making it the category with both the highest revenue and highest booking volume. Salon for Women had 65 bookings, the lowest booking volume among the categories, while generating ₹151,689 in revenue.
Implication:
For the Category lead, Deep Home Cleaning represents the largest current revenue and booking base. Salon for Women has the lowest booking volume, so its demand generation and booking conversion are areas to examine if the goal is to increase category volume.
